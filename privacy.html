Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who uses this app

AI Video Studio is used only by its owner, to manage the owner's own YouTube channels (Throttle Bulletin and The Gaming Detour). It is not offered to other people.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner connects a channel, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • read the channel's name and ID, to confirm the right channel is connected,
  • upload videos and edit their titles, descriptions and privacy settings,
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics, to see how videos perform.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any other server.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

AI Video Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The owner can also delete the stored tokens from the computer.
